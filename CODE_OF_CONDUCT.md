# Plandillo Code of Conduct

Everyone is welcome to ask questions, report problems and contribute to Plandillo, regardless of their background or experience. This code applies to contributors and maintainers in project spaces, including issues, pull requests and discussions. It also applies when someone officially represents the project elsewhere.

## Working together

- In a review, explain what you think could go wrong and suggest a change or a test. Give the author room to ask questions or disagree.
- Treat people with respect across differences in identity, language, ability and experience. A first-time contributor should feel comfortable asking how something works.
- Respect personal boundaries. Harassment, discriminatory remarks, threats, sexual advances and repeated unwanted contact are unacceptable. Sharing someone’s private information without permission or retaliating against someone who reports a concern is also unacceptable.

## Reporting a concern

If you experience or witness a violation, email Huntly Digital privately at [plandillo@huntly.digital](mailto:plandillo@huntly.digital). Describe what happened and include a link if you have one. Share only the details you are comfortable disclosing. Keep sensitive reports out of public issues and pull requests.

For abuse on GitHub, you can also report directly to GitHub Support, including when a maintainer is involved. See [GitHub’s abuse reporting process](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).

## How maintainers respond

Maintainers review reports privately and share details only with people who need them to handle the report. A maintainer who is the subject of a report does not decide its outcome.

Depending on the severity and whether the behavior continues, maintainers may ask someone to change their conduct, remove content, or temporarily or permanently restrict participation.
